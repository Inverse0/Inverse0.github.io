I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Duke University, advised by [Prof. Sudeepa Roy](https://users.cs.duke.edu/~sudeepa/) and [Prof. Jun Yang](https://users.cs.duke.edu/~junyang/). My current work focuses on SQL validation and relational program verification.

I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Columbia University in December 2024, where I worked with [Prof. Kenneth Ross](https://www.cs.columbia.edu/~kar/), [Prof. Henning Schulzrinne](https://www.cs.columbia.edu/~hgs/), and [Prof. Eugene Wu](https://www.eugenewu.net/). Before that, I earned my B.A. in Computer Science and Economics from Boston University in May 2023, where I worked with [Prof. Manos Athanassoulis](https://cs-people.bu.edu/mathan/).
